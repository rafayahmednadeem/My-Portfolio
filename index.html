(02) About

Raw footage in.
Watchable video out.

I'm Rafay Ahmed. I edit gaming videos, YouTube Shorts and social clips.

Most of my cutting happens in CapCut Pro. Covers, thumbnails and graphics come out of Canva. I like finding the part of a long clip that people actually want to watch, and building the edit around it.

I'm still learning and still getting faster. Every project teaches me something I use on the next one.

Tools
CapCut Pro, Canva
Formats

(03) Services

What I do

  1. A

    Gaming edits

    Montages, highlights and full videos. I trim the dead time, match cuts to the audio and keep the best moments on screen.

  2. B

    Shorts and Reels

    Vertical cuts built for the first three seconds. Captions, quick pacing, clean exports for each platform.

  3. C

    Thumbnails and covers

    Made in Canva to match the video, so the thumbnail and the edit look like they belong together.

  4. D

    Revisions

    You send notes, I fix them. Tell me what to change and I'll turn it around without a long back-and-forth.
